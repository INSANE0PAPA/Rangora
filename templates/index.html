Campus Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about pass booking, live Seat Pulse, attendance check-ins, vouchers, and festival policies.

Passes are reserved with ACID transaction safety in our Neon PostgreSQL relational database. Seat Pulse dynamically calculates real-time capacity (Seats Available, Almost Full, or Registration Closed — Full) based on confirmed registrations vs. the physical venue seating limit.
Registering confirms your seat and generates an alphanumeric digital ticket pass (RNG-XXXX-XXXX). Verified attendance occurs when the authorized event organizer scans and checks you in at the campus door. Attendance stamps and attendance-based achievements are awarded exclusively upon verified check-in.
Platform administrators launch limited-quantity academic voucher campaigns (e.g. 50% discount, free workshop pass, VIP access). When issued to you, vouchers appear under "My Rewards & Vouchers" in your dashboard. You can copy the code or click "Redeem" to claim the pass perk for an eligible session. Each voucher is single-use and strictly tracked.
Plan Rescue inspects your saved events and automatically detects timestamp clashes. When a conflict occurs, it recommends conflict-free alternative sessions from the same festival and provides a 1-click swap button to effortlessly optimize your itinerary.
Custom Achievement Passes are 100% optional. Campus organizers can optionally create them to reward attendees who complete specific challenges (e.g. attending all robotics hackathons or multi-day craft ateliers). If an organizer chooses not to create a pass, participants still earn standard Festival DNA stats and platform accolades.
Yes. You can cancel any confirmed registration from your Participant Dashboard before the event starts. The database instantly updates the registration status to cancelled and replenishes available seats in real time.
